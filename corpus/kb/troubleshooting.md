# Troubleshooting

## App will not load
First, refresh the page. If it still fails, sign out and sign back in. If the
problem continues, clear the browser cache. Most loading problems are fixed by
these three steps.

## Sync is slow or stuck
Sync can slow down when many changes are made at once. Wait two minutes and
refresh. If a single item is stuck, open it, make a small edit, and save again
to force a re-sync.

## Error code E-402
Error code E-402 means the session has expired. Sign out and sign back in to
clear it. This is not a billing error, even though the number looks like one.
