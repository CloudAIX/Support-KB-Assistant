# Shipping and Delivery (Hardware Add-ons)

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within the country. Express
delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. International delivery takes 7 to 14
business days.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when an order ships. Tracking updates can take up
to 24 hours to appear after the email is sent.

## Damaged or missing items
Report a damaged or missing item within 7 days of delivery. A replacement is
sent at no cost once the report is confirmed.
