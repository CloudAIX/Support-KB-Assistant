# Billing and Refunds

## Refund window
Customers can request a refund within 14 days of a charge. Refunds are
processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days
after approval.

## What is not refundable
Add-on credits that have already been used are not refundable. Annual plans
cancelled after the 14-day window are not refunded for the remainder of the
term, but the plan stays active until the period ends.

## How a refund is approved
A refund is approved automatically if the request is inside the 14-day window
and the charge is for a standard plan. Requests outside the window go to a
support agent for review.
