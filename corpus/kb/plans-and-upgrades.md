# Plans and Upgrades

## Plan types
There are three plans: Starter, Team, and Business. Starter is for one user.
Team covers up to 10 users. Business has no user limit and adds priority
support.

## Upgrade or downgrade
Upgrades take effect straight away and the customer is charged the difference
for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the
next billing period, so the customer keeps the higher plan until then.

## Seats
A seat is one user on a Team or Business plan. Seats can be added at any time.
Removed seats free up at the next billing period.
