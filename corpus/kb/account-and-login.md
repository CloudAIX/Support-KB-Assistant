# Account and Login

## Reset a password
Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. A reset email arrives
within a few minutes. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes.

## Locked account
An account locks after 5 failed sign-in attempts. The lock clears
automatically after 30 minutes, or a support agent can clear it sooner after
verifying the account holder.

## Change the email on an account
The account owner can change the sign-in email under Settings, then Profile.
A confirmation link is sent to the new address. The change is not complete
until that link is clicked.
